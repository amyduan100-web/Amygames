# Amygames

Free little browser games by Amy. Play at https://amy.94me.fyi

## Games

### Good Witch 🧙‍♀️🏝️
A thunderstorm broke the good witch's wand and broom. She fell onto a monster island and hit the ground so hard that she forgot all her spells except 🔥 Fire. Ten masters of magic live on the islands nearby. Help each master with a mission and they'll teach you a spell. Then buy a new broom and fly home!

**Controls**
- **Move**: WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, drag on the left half of the screen.
- **Cast a spell**: hold the mouse to aim and charge, then let go. On a phone, hold on the right half of the screen.
- **Change spell**: number keys 1–9, 0 and -, or Q / E, the mouse wheel, or the buttons at the bottom of the screen.
- **Magic shots**: you have 💫 30, and every spell you cast uses one. When they run out, click the witch (or press **R**). She asks "What spell do you want to do?" Pick one and enter its password from memory to get 30 shots again. If you forget, there's a "Peek in notebook 📓" button.
- **H**: drink a 🧪 potion.
- **P**: pause.
- The 📜 mission line in the top-left corner always says what to do next.

**Home Island**
- 🏠 **Your house**: you can sleep in the evening or at night. Sleeping brings your hearts back and saves the game.
  - With the house shield, monsters can't come near the house. You also sleep really well and gain +1 max heart each night, up to 10.
- 🏘️ **Village**: monsters can't get in. The shop sells:
  - 🧪 Potion: 15 coins
  - 🛡️ House shield crystal: 60 coins
  - 🔮 New wand, which makes every spell do +1 damage: 150 coins
  - 🧹 New broom, which flies you home and wins the game: 300 coins
- Monsters chase you and drop 💰 coins. Every island has lots of them, Home Island too. At night more come out, including wolves.

**Flying to other islands**
- **Hold any spell's key, or press and hold its button, for 3 seconds.** A ring fills up around the witch while you wait. If a monster hits you, the flight spell is cancelled.
- Pick an island from the map.
- How she flies depends on the spell:
  - 🔥 Fire, ☄️ Meteor or ⭐ Star: blasts off like a rocket
  - ⚡ Lightning: zaps there in a flash
  - 🌪️ Tornado: spins away
  - Every other spell: floats
- **In the sky, flying monsters try to attack you!** They turn toward wherever the witch is. **Move your mouse to dodge**, because the witch follows it. On a phone she follows your finger, and the arrow keys work too. Each hit costs a heart. The harder the island you're flying to, the more monsters there are and the faster they aim.

**Spell passwords**
- Every spell has a secret password made of magic runes: 🌙 ⭐ 💧 🍃 🔥 💎.
- At the very start, the witch tries to remember her spells and remembers 🔥 Fire, whose password is 🔥⭐🔥.
- In a lesson, press **Learn ✨** and the master shows the spell's password one rune at a time. Click the runes back in the same order (keys 1–6 work too). If you get one wrong, the master shows it again.
- Stronger spells have longer passwords, from 3 runes up to 5.
- 📓 **Spell notebook**: every time you learn a spell, the witch writes its password into her notebook with a ✏️ pencil, and the notebook flies up to the 📓 button at the top. Click that button any time to see the passwords of the spells you've learned.

**The ten masters**
Each master lives on their own island and asks for help. Once you've helped, they teach you their spell. The islands go from easiest to hardest. Stronger islands have tougher monsters (more hearts and scarier types) and a stronger master with a stronger spell. An island unlocks after you've helped the master on the island before it. A mission island shows up on your island map when a master sends you there.

**Out of hearts?** There's no Game Over. The witch wakes up in her own bed and thinks it was all a dream. She flies off on her broom, then lightning strikes again and she crashes back onto the island. The adventure starts over from the beginning, again and again!

**Winning**: buy the new broom and fly home. Can you learn all 11 spells first?

### Potion Shop 🍄🧪
You decide to open a potion shop in the village. You don't have a house yet, so you live in a tiny bedroom in the back of the shop!

- **Walk around**: move the witch with **W A S D** or the arrow keys (or click a place) on the store map and the town map. Walk into the counter to work, into the 🚻 washroom, into your 🛏️ bedroom, or out the 🚪 door to town. In town, walk into a building to go inside.
- **Your needs**: 🚽 about every 50 seconds of work, a **"Quick!"** sign pops up: you need the washroom! Press 🏃 to run there, or wait a little. About 25 seconds later it's MUST GO, and you can't make potions until you go. 🥱 When night is coming, you get sleepy, so go to bed!
- **The store map**: customers walk in through the 🚪 door and ask for help at the counter. For example: "Hello! I fell off my bike yesterday and scraped my knee. Can you help me?"
- **Make it**: find the answer in your 📖 recipe book, click the ingredients into the cauldron **in the right order**, and press ✨ Make it! Orders can be:
  - plain food and drinks (💧 🥛 🍎 🍞 🍪)
  - magic foods (🌟 Star Cookie, 😂 Giggle Milk…)
  - potions (💗 Healing, 🕊️ Flying, ✨ Wish…)
- **No rush**: customers wait 2 minutes and give a 💡 hint after a while.
- **After they're served**, happy customers sit at a table to eat and drink, sometimes visit the 🚻 washroom, and sometimes leave a 💰 tip you can click.
- **Supplies run out**: every ingredient has a count. Buy more in 🗺️ town.
- **The town**:
  - 🛒 Grocery for food and drinks
  - 🌿 Magic Herb Shop for magic ingredients
  - 📚 Recipe Library for new recipes
  - 🎀 Decor Store for decorations
  - 🔨 Builder to make your shop bigger (🏪 → 🏬 → ✨)
  - 💼 Job Center to hire helpers
  - 🏠 your home
- **Be the boss**: hire helpers (👩‍🍳 Mia, 👨‍🍳 Leo) at the Job Center. Each one serves 3 of her own customers a day and gives you the money. When a helper is working, she also serves the customers who would come to your counter, so the counter only shows a ❗ when someone needs you (like a helper asking for a recipe or supplies). Each helper has a little tab at the top of the shop (like "👩‍🍳 Mia 🥄") that shows what she's doing. Click it for her pop-up with 📖 Teach, 🙋 Take over and 🏖️ PA day.
  - **📖 Teach**: she starts out knowing only food and drinks. Teach her your recipes anytime: she takes out her 📓 notebook, writes the recipe down with a ✏️ pencil and says "Got it, boss!". If a customer wants something she doesn't know, she asks you for the recipe. If nobody knows it, she asks you to buy it.
  - **📦 Supplies**: when something runs out, she asks you to go to town and buy more.
  - **💰 Payday every 3 work days**: Mia gets 💰60 and Leo gets 💰100. She comes and asks you. If you don't pay (or can't), she quits!
  - **🏖️ PA day**: give her tomorrow off. Her customers come to your counter instead, and it doesn't count toward payday.
  - **🙋 Take over**: let her rest and serve her customers yourself. Press **👋 Let her come back** anytime, and she takes back the customers you haven't served yet.
  - **🍽️ Lunch**: helpers take turns eating lunch (Mia after her 1st customer, Leo after his 2nd, never at the same time), so customers are never missed.
- **Your lunch**: about a minute into the day, an **"I'm hungry!"** sign pops up. You can wait a little bit, but about 40 seconds later it's **"I'm STARVING!"** and you have to go eat. Eat 🍞 bread and an 🍎 apple from your shelf (or a 🥪 sandwich from home) to feel less sleepy. Your helpers keep serving while you eat.
- **Home**: it grows in three steps:
  1. 🛏️ A tiny bedroom in your shop.
  2. 🏠 Your own **Little House** (💰300), with a kitchen, a living room with a sofa and TV, a bedroom and a bathroom.
  3. 🏰 The **Big Mansion**: sell your little house to move in. It has a pool, a garden, a piano and more.

  Every home is a map you can click things in (cook pancakes, watch TV, take a bubble bath…). Click the bed to sleep. Once your shop is the Grand Potion Store, you can also buy a 🎪 Play House full of toys.

### Richest 🎒💰
You start with nothing: just an old backpack and a bench in the park. Can you become the richest person in town?

- **The city map** 🗺️ shows 9 blocks. Click a block to go there.
- **Each block has its own little streets.** Click anywhere to walk there, even on an empty spot. Click a building to go inside. The 🗺️ City map button takes you back.
- **Cans** 🥫 lie on the street corners, and people walking by toss new ones! Pick them up and bring them to ♻️ Recycling on Clean Street for 💰0.50 each.
- **Your needs**: 🍞 hunger, 😴 energy and 🧼 clean. When you're homeless, nobody cares if you're stinky! But the ☕ café, 🍝 restaurant and 🏥 hospital jobs need you to be clean, so pay 💰3 for a shower at the 🚿 Bath House first. Once you buy a home, when it says "you're getting dirty", take a free 🛁 bath at home.
  - **Hungry?** A pop-up tells you and opens your 🎒 backpack so you can eat. No food? Go to the 🥫 Food Bank for free soup, or the 🛒 Grocery Store.
  - **Starving?** You have 60 seconds to eat something, or it's game over and you start again on the park bench!
  - **Too tired?** You lie down right where you are and sleep.
  - You can sleep on the 🌳 park bench, or in your own 🏠 home once you buy one.
- **Money**:
  - Start by collecting cans.
  - Then get jobs at the 💼 Job Center. You can have **up to 3 jobs** at the same time, and quit one anytime:
    - 🧹 Street Sweeper, 🐶 Dog Walker, 🧽 Car Washer
    - 📰 Newspaper Delivery (needs a 🚲 bike)
    - 🚌 Bus Driver (needs 🎓 class 1)
    - ☕ Café Helper, 🧁 Baker (must be clean)
    - 👮 Police Officer (needs 🎓 class 2)
    - 👩‍🍳 Chef (needs 🎓 class 1)
    - 👩‍🏫 Teacher: answer math questions (needs 🎓 class 3)
    - 🎸 Rock Star (needs 🎓 class 2)
    - 🏦 Banker (needs 🎓 class 3)
    - 👩‍⚕️ Doctor (needs 🎓 class 3)
  - 👑 **Become a leader**: when everything you own is worth 💰50,000, you can become the 🎩 **Mayor** at the Town Hall (💰1,000 every morning). At 💰100,000, you can become the 👑 **Queen** (💰3,000 every morning)!
- **Things to buy**:
  - food, a 🚲 bike and a 🚗 car at the 🛒 Grocery Store. Food goes into your 🎒 backpack. Click **🎒 Backpack** at the top to see what you have and eat it. Better food keeps you full for longer: 🍎 1 hour, 🥪 2 hours, 🍱 4 hours, 🍖 8 hours. If you get too hungry with food in your backpack, you eat it on your own.
  - 🎓 classes at the 🏫 School
  - homes (🏢 → 🏡 → 🏰) and businesses (🍋 🏪 🏨 🏙️) at 🏦 Homes & Business. Businesses make money for you every morning.
- **Visit places as a customer**: every workplace has a 🙋 Visit part, and a 💼 Work part if it's your job.
  - 🎠 a free playground at the 🌳 Park: swings, slide, sandbox and climbing bars
  - 🏥 checkup
  - 🍝 a meal at the restaurant
  - ☕ hot cocoa and 🍩 donuts at the café
  - 🚗 a car wash (if you have a car or bike)
  - 📰 a newspaper with funny news
- **Your home** is a little map with rooms, like the Potion Shop houses. Walk around and click things: 🛏️ bed to sleep, 🛁 bath, 🍳 stove (cook food from your backpack), 📺 TV, 🎮 games and more.
  - 🎒 Leave your backpack on the hook by the door if you want. But then you can't pick up cans or buy groceries until you go home and take it again!
- **The big life**: in the 🏰 Mansion you can eat from your giant fridge for free. If you're a 👩‍⚕️ Doctor, 🎸 Rock Star or 🏦 Banker, you can also stay home and 💻 work from home on your computer.
- **🐾 Pets**: adopt a 🐶 dog or a 🐱 cat at the Pet Shop on Home Lane. Your pet follows you everywhere! Buy 🦴 dog food or 🐟 cat food, and a 🎾 toy. They go in your backpack, so you can feed your pet and play with it. Your pet tells you when it's hungry or bored.
- **Win**: check the 🏆 Richest list at the 🏛️ Town Hall. Get to #1!

## Emojis
All the games show the same emoji pictures on every computer, iPad and phone. They come from [Twemoji](https://github.com/jdecked/twemoji) (CC-BY 4.0). The good witch has her own picture (`assets/witch.svg`), based on Twemoji, with long brown hair and a blue headband. The code that does this is in `js/emoji.js`.

## Play on your computer
Open `index.html` in a web browser. You don't need to install anything.

## Adding a new game
1. Make a new folder `games/<game-name>/` with its own `index.html`.
2. Add a new card for it in the root `index.html`.
