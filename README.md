# Amygames

Free little browser games by Amy. Play at https://amy.94me.fyi

## Games

### Good Witch 🧙‍♀️🏝️
A thunderstorm broke the good witch's wand and broom. She fell onto a monster island and hit the ground so hard that she forgot all her spells except 🔥 Fire. Ten masters of magic live on the islands nearby. Help each master with a mission and they'll teach you a spell. Then buy a new broom and fly home!

**Controls**
- **Move**: WASD or the arrow keys. On a phone, drag on the left half of the screen.
- **Cast a spell**: hold the mouse to aim and charge, then let go. On a phone, hold on the right half of the screen.
- **Change spell**: number keys 1–9, 0 and -, or Q / E, the mouse wheel, or the buttons at the bottom of the screen.
- **Magic shots**: you have 💫 30, and every spell you cast uses one. When they run out, click the witch (or press **R**). She asks "What spell do you want to do?" Pick one and enter its password from memory to get 30 shots again. If you forget, there's a "Peek in notebook 📓" button.
- **H**: drink a 🧪 potion.
- **P**: pause.
- The 📜 mission line in the top-left corner always says what to do next.

**Home Island**
- 🏠 **Your house**: you can sleep in the evening or at night. Sleeping brings your hearts back and saves the game.
  - With the house shield, monsters can't come near the house. You also sleep really well and gain +1 max heart each night, up to 10.
- 🏘️ **Village**: monsters can't get in. The shop sells:
  - 🧪 Potion: 15 coins
  - 🛡️ House shield crystal: 60 coins
  - 🔮 New wand, which makes every spell do +1 damage: 150 coins
  - 🧹 New broom, which flies you home and wins the game: 300 coins
- Monsters chase you and drop 💰 coins. Every island has lots of them, Home Island too. At night more come out, including wolves.

**Flying to other islands**
- **Hold any spell's key, or press and hold its button, for 3 seconds.** A ring fills up around the witch while you wait. If a monster hits you, the flight spell is cancelled.
- Pick an island from the map.
- How she flies depends on the spell:
  - 🔥 Fire, ☄️ Meteor or ⭐ Star: blasts off like a rocket
  - ⚡ Lightning: zaps there in a flash
  - 🌪️ Tornado: spins away
  - Every other spell: floats
- **In the sky, flying monsters try to attack you!** They turn toward wherever the witch is. **Move your mouse to dodge**, because the witch follows it. On a phone she follows your finger, and the arrow keys work too. Each hit costs a heart. The harder the island you're flying to, the more monsters there are and the faster they aim.

**Spell passwords**
- Every spell has a secret password made of magic runes: 🌙 ⭐ 💧 🍃 🔥 💎.
- At the very start, the witch tries to remember her spells and remembers 🔥 Fire, whose password is 🔥⭐🔥.
- In a lesson, the master asks "Which power do you want to learn?" and you click the spell.
- The master shows its password one rune at a time. Click the runes back in the same order (keys 1–6 work too). If you get one wrong, the master shows it again.
- Stronger spells have longer passwords, from 3 runes up to 5.
- 📓 **Spell notebook**: every time you learn a spell, the witch writes its password into her notebook with a ✏️ pencil, and the notebook flies up to the 📓 button at the top. Click that button any time to see the passwords of the spells you've learned.

**The ten masters**
Each master lives on their own island and asks for help. Once you've helped, they teach you their spell. The islands go from easiest to hardest. Stronger islands have tougher monsters (more hearts and scarier types) and a stronger master with a stronger spell. An island unlocks after you've helped the master on the island before it. A mission island shows up on your island map when a master sends you there.

**Out of hearts?** There's no Game Over. The witch wakes up in her own bed and thinks it was all a dream. She flies off on her broom, then lightning strikes again and she crashes back onto the island. The adventure starts over from the beginning, again and again!

**Winning**: buy the new broom and fly home. Can you learn all 11 spells first?

## Play on your computer
Open `index.html` in a web browser. You don't need to install anything.

## Adding a new game
1. Make a new folder `games/<game-name>/` with its own `index.html`.
2. Add a new card for it in the root `index.html`.
