Richest

You have nothing but an old backpack. You sleep on a bench in the park. But you have a big dream: to become the richest person in town!

  • The 🗺️ city map shows all the blocks. Click a block to go there.
  • Each block has its own little streets. Click anywhere to walk, and click a building to go inside.
  • Look for cans 🥫 on the street corners. People toss them on the ground! Pick them up and bring them to ♻️ Recycling for 💰1 each.
  • Go to the 💼 Job Center to choose a job. Better jobs need school 🎓, a bike 🚲 or a shower 🚿 first.
  • 🍞 Hungry? Buy food, or if you have no money, the 🥫 Food Bank helps you for free. If you get too hungry, you go there on your own.
  • 😴 Too tired? You just lie down wherever you are and sleep. Buy a home 🏠 to sleep in a real bed!
  • Buy homes, a car and businesses, and climb the 🏆 Richest list at the 🏛️ Town Hall until you're #1!
