Good Witch

The good witch fell on a monster island and forgot her spells. Survive, help 10 masters, learn 10 spells and fly home!

  • Move: W A S D or arrow keys. On a phone, drag on the left side.
  • Cast a spell: hold the mouse to aim and charge, let go to cast. On a phone, hold on the right side.
  • Magic shots: you have 💫 30. When they run out, click the witch (or press R), pick a spell and enter its password to recharge.
  • Change spell: number keys, Q / E, or the buttons at the bottom. H drinks a 🧪 potion.
  • Fly to other islands: hold a spell's key (or its button) for 3 seconds. 🔥 Fire launches you like a rocket! Don't get hit while you wait.
  • Help each master with a mission (often helping villagers in trouble!), and they'll teach you a spell.
  • Monsters drop 💰 coins for the village shop. A 🧹 new broom takes you home!
  • Night is dangerous. Sleep in your 🏠 house to heal and save your game.
  • Careful! If you run out of hearts, the witch wakes up in bed... was it all a dream? Then it all happens again from the start!
