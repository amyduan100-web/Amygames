Good Witch

Monsters leap into the forest and chase the good witch! Help her fight back.

  • Move: W A S D or the arrow keys. On a phone, drag on the left side.
  • Fireball: hold the mouse to aim, like pulling back a bow. Hold longer for a stronger, faster shot, then let go to fire. On a phone, hold on the right side.
  • Each monster has ❤️ hearts. Every hit takes one away.
  • Watch out: 🦇 bats and 👻 ghosts can dodge! Trees block fireballs.
  • Press P to pause.
