Potion Shop

One day you have a big idea: "I'm going to open a potion shop!" You don't have a house yet, so you make a tiny bedroom in the back of your shop and live there. Villagers will come in with problems, and you'll brew the potion that helps.

  • Read what the customer needs, then find the right potion in your 📖 recipe book.
  • Click the ingredients into the cauldron in the right order, then press ✨ Make it!
  • Sometimes people just want normal food or a drink 🍎💧, and sometimes a magic food 🌟🍪!
  • Customers sit at your tables to eat and drink, and sometimes leave a 💰 tip. Click it!
  • Your supplies run out! Walk into 🗺️ town to buy food at the Grocery and magic stuff at the Herb Shop.
  • In town you can also buy recipes at the Library, decorations, a bigger shop (🏪 → 🏬 → ✨), and hire helpers.
  • Hire helpers 👩‍🍳 to serve customers for you. They'll come to you, the boss, when they need help.
  • Customers wait up to 2 minutes, so take your time! After a little while they give you a 💡 hint.
  • Walk the witch with W A S D (or the arrow keys), or click a place. Walk to the counter to work, to the 🚻 washroom when you need to go, and out the 🚪 door to town.
  • A sign pops up when you need the 🚽 washroom or when you're 😋 hungry. When night comes you'll get 🥱 sleepy, so go to bed!
  • At night, sleep in your 🛏️ bedroom in the shop. Save up to buy your own 🏠 little house!
  • With lots and lots of money, sell your little house and move into a 🏰 big mansion!
  • Once your shop is the biggest, you can also buy a 🎪 Play House full of toys.
